# 🎹 Ableton Agent Studio for macOS

A visual, 1-click desktop companion for controlling **Ableton Live 11/12 with AI**. Designed for musicians and producers — zero command-line or technical experience needed.

![Studio Preview](minis://attachments/ableton-studio-preview.jpg)

## 🚀 How to Run on macOS (Zero Setup)

### Option 1: Double-Click the App
1. Open the folder in Finder.
2. Double-click **`AbletonAgent.app`** (or **`AbletonAgent.command`**).
3. The Studio dashboard automatically opens in your browser!

### Option 2: Via Terminal (If preferred)
```bash
pip install --upgrade ableton-agent
ableton-agent app
```

---

## ⚡ What You Can Do in 1 Click

1. **Auto-Install Remote Script:**
   - Just click the glowing **"⚡ Install Script"** button at the top right.
   - It automatically places `ChatGPTBridge` into your Mac's `~/Library/Preferences/Ableton/Live 11 or 12/User Remote Scripts/` folder.
   - Inside Ableton: go to **Preferences > Link, Tempo & MIDI** and choose **ChatGPTBridge** under Control Surface. That's it!

2. **AI Music Producer:**
   - Pick your AI provider with 1 tap: **OpenAI (GPT-4o)**, **Ollama (100% Free & Local)**, **LM Studio**, **Groq (Instant speed)**, or **DeepSeek**.
   - Click preset chips like **Tech House**, **Lo-Fi Chill**, **Dark Ambient**, or **Afrobeat**, or describe your own track.
   - Click **"🚀 Build in Ableton Live"** and watch your project build itself in real time!

3. **Music Theory & Groove Sequencer:**
   - **Harmonic Chord Generator**: Select root note and chord type (min7, maj7, min9, sus4) and hit **"Send Chord to Live"**.
   - **16-Step Euclidean Sequencer**: Adjust the Pulses and Swing sliders to generate humanized Afrobeat, House, or Techno polyrhythms and send them directly to track clips.

4. **Live Project Inspector:**
   - Click **"🔍 Inspect Set"** to see all active tracks, instruments, loaded audio effects, and tempo live from your active Ableton session.

5. **Claude & Cursor MCP Integration:**
   - Click **"📋 Copy Claude Desktop Config"** and paste it into your Claude Desktop or Cursor configuration to chat with Ableton directly.
